## The short answer

**The B.A.A. does not set a minimum.** There is no single dollar figure to "enter the Boston Marathon
via charity." Each nonprofit in the Bank of America Boston Marathon Official Charity Program receives
a block of invitational entries and **sets its own fundraising minimum**.

Practical planning numbers, based on published amounts:

| If you… | Expect to commit |
| --- | --- |
| Need a bib from a charity (no qualifying time) | **$7,500 – $15,000**, now clustered at **$12,000** |
| Already have your own B.A.A. bib (qualifier) and join a team | **$1,000 – $5,400**, clustered at **$3,000 – $4,000** |
| Are registered and want the B.A.A.'s own Climate Crew | **$1,500** |

> **If fundraising $10,000 isn't viable, read the International Tour Operator section below
> first.** Official B.A.A. tour operators hold contingents for runners who don't meet the qualifying
> standard — no qualifying time, no fundraising, no personal liability. Hotel + guaranteed bib without
> flights runs **€2,135–2,700 / ~CHF 2,710**; the bib component alone is **€700–CHF 950**.

**Minimums rose for 2027.** Where a team has published a 2027 figure it is higher than its 2026 one:
Trinity Boston Connects went **$10,000 → $12,000** and Dana-Farber confirmed **$12,000**. Treat $12,000,
not $10,000, as the working number for a competitive team.

On top of the fundraising commitment you pay the B.A.A. entry fee yourself. **The 2027 fee is $390**,
per Trinity Boston Connects quoting the B.A.A.-set amount in its application pack. Some teams add their
own administrative fee on top — Trinity charges **$100** for application processing and gear if accepted.

## How the commitment actually works

- **You pay the B.A.A. entry fee separately** from the fundraising minimum. It is not covered by donations.
- **The minimum is a guarantee, not a goal.** Teams take a credit card authorization and charge you the
  shortfall if you miss the number by the deadline (typically mid-May, ~4 weeks post-race).
- **Interim milestones are common** — e.g. Trinity Boston Connects requires $3,000 by December.
- **Entries are non-deferrable.** If you cannot run, the fundraising obligation generally still stands.
- **Higher pledges win bibs.** Several teams (Mass Eye and Ear, Trinity Boston Connects) explicitly give
  application preference to runners who commit above the stated minimum.
- **Qualifying first is the cheapest route onto a team** — own-bib minimums run 60–90% lower.

## Cheaper route for non-qualifiers: official International Tour Operators

> **Correction, September 2026: these places are rationed, not simply purchasable.** Sports Tours
> International declined a 2027 Boston request outright, writing that "our allocation for the Boston
> Marathon is significantly smaller than for the other Abbott World Marathon Majors, meaning we have to
> carefully prioritise places based on customers' booking history" — and that **all** their successful
> 2027 Boston runners "have travelled with us on nine or more events." Boston is the one Major where
> operator entries go to loyal repeat customers first. Treat the prices below as what a place costs
> *if offered one*, not as an open purchase. Apply early and expect to be turned down.

The B.A.A. authorizes [40 official International Tour Operators](https://www.baa.org/races/boston-marathon/info-for-athletes-international-tour-operators/)
(9 Asia, 20 Europe, 3 Oceania, 8 Americas). They receive an allocation of guaranteed entries requiring
**no qualifying time and no fundraising** — the bib comes bundled into a travel package.

Worked example — [Endeavor Maratones Internacionales](https://endeavortravel.com/maratones/boston/),
the official operator for **Spain and Portugal** (listed on the B.A.A. page as "Portugan", a typo):

| Component | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Guaranteed bib ("Dorsal garantizado para el corredor") | **€700** |
| Hotel-only package, 4 nights, quad room | €1,435 pp |
| Hotel-only package, 4 nights, double room | €1,985 pp |
| Hotel-only package, 4 nights, single room | €2,830 |
| Flight + hotel package, 4 nights, double room | €2,810 pp |

**Floor for hotel + bib, sharing a room: roughly €2,135–2,685, flights excluded.** Prices are from the
2026 edition (April 17, 2026 departure); 2027 pricing will differ but the structure is stable. Endeavor
also splits payment into four installments (€800 + bib on booking, then €800 by Sept 30, €800 by Dec 31,
balance by Feb 28).

Compare: **€700 marginal cost for the bib, with no personal liability**, versus a **$7,500–$15,000
fundraising commitment backed by your credit card**. If you'd be paying for a Boston hotel regardless,
the tour operator route is dramatically cheaper.

Conditions and caveats:

- **Operators are country-scoped and generally restricted to residents of that country.** The B.A.A.
  describes Active Holiday Company, for instance, as permitted to offer guaranteed entries "to Indian
  and UAE residents only." Check your own country's operator on the B.A.A. list.
- **The bib cannot be bought on its own** — the B.A.A. requires operators to bundle entry with
  accommodation, which is what sets the effective floor.
- **You register yourself.** Per the B.A.A.: "all participants must submit their own individual event
  registration and sign the event participant waiver; tour operators cannot submit registration on the
  participant's behalf." The operator supplies the entry; the paperwork is yours.
- Allocations are finite and sell out; these packages typically go on sale well before race year.

### Swiss residents (and dual Swiss/Portuguese nationals)

Switzerland has **three** operators on the B.A.A. list, and a Portuguese passport opens the Iberian
operators as a second channel. Of the three Swiss entries, only Albis Reisen has a live Boston product:
DER Touristik Suisse's link (`reise-sport.ch`) now redirects to a dead Kuoni path, and Tourisme Pour Tous
(`hotelplan-suisse.ch`) shows no Boston offering.

[**Albis Reisen**](https://www.albisreisen.ch/reisen/detail/marathon-ultras/boston-marathon/) states the
key fact outright: *"Wir haben ein kleines Kontingent für Läufer, welche die Qualifikationskriterien
nicht erfüllen"* — a small contingent for runners who don't meet the qualifying criteria. Their price
list separates qualified from non-qualified runners, which isolates the cost of the bib itself:

| Sheraton Boston, 4 nights, double | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Non-qualified runner** (bib included) | CHF 3,710 |
| Qualified runner or non-runner (no bib) | CHF 2,760 |
| **→ Implied cost of the guaranteed bib** | **CHF 950** |
| Single occupancy, non-qualified | CHF 4,670 |
| Reduction for dropping SWISS flights + airport transfers | **−CHF 1,000 pp** |

**Hotel + bib, no flights, shared double, 4 nights: CHF 3,710 − 1,000 = ~CHF 2,710.**

Three-way comparison for hotel + guaranteed bib, 4 nights, flights excluded:

| Operator | Country scope | Hotel + bib | Bib alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Endeavor](https://endeavortravel.com/maratones/boston/) | Spain, Portugal | **€2,135** (quad) / €2,265 (triple) / €2,685 (double) | **€700** |
| [Geosport](https://geosport.es/viajes/maraton-de-boston/) | Spain | €2,695 (double king), €2,845 (double-double) | bundled |
| [Albis Reisen](https://www.albisreisen.ch/reisen/detail/marathon-ultras/boston-marathon/) | Switzerland | ~CHF 2,710 (double) | CHF 950 |

The three cluster tightly around **€2,650–2,700 / CHF 2,710** for a shared double — independent
confirmation that this is the real market rate. Endeavor's triple and quad rooms are the cheapest route
found anywhere at **€2,135–2,265**, and Endeavor's bib is the cheapest single component at €700
(~CHF 650) versus Albis's CHF 950.

Caveats specific to this route:

- **Only Geosport's page is confirmed 2027 priced.** Albis Reisen says *"Wir informieren in Kürze über
  unser Programm fürs 2027"* — 2027 not yet published, and their page still shows the 2026 qualifier
  window (Sept 8–12, 2025) rather than the 2027 one. Endeavor's figures are 2026-dated.
- **Eligibility may be residency-based, not nationality-based.** Whether a Swiss resident with a
  Portuguese passport can buy through Endeavor is the open question — worth asking both, since it is
  worth a few hundred francs.
- **Contingents are small and sell out**, and the bib cannot be unbundled from accommodation.

## Method and confidence

- Race date, program criteria, LOI window and roster count come from the B.A.A. directly.
- Figures marked "confirmed by email" come from the charity's own written reply, quoted in
  [What the charities actually said](#what-the-charities-actually-said). These supersede earlier
  website-derived figures, including two the site data got wrong or could not resolve: Dana-Farber's
  invitational minimum (unpublished on their FAQ, now confirmed at $12,000) and Trinity's minimum
  (published as $10,000 for 2026, now $12,000 for 2027).
- Every other dollar figure was read off the named charity's own page — no figure rests on a
  search-result summary alone. Where a figure could not be confirmed it is marked "not published" or
  "TBC" rather than estimated.
- The September 2026 correction to the tour operator section came from a written rejection, not
  inference: the earlier version of this document overstated how purchasable those places are.
- Aggregator sites circulating claims like "27 official charity partners" or "minimums start at $750"
  are wrong or unsourced; the official program had 193 members for 2026 and the lowest confirmed
  charity-bib minimum found was $7,500.
- 2025 program total raised: $50.4 million; more than $600 million raised since the program began in 1989.
